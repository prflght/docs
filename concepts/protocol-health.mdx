Prflght continuously monitors the health of the DeFi protocols your agent interacts with. Before approving any transaction, Prflght checks the current health of every protocol involved and can block the transaction if a protocol is under stress. This protects your agent from executing trades on venues that may be experiencing an exploit, a liquidity crisis, or other adverse conditions.

## Monitored protocols

Prflght currently monitors 12 venues: Orca, Drift, Kamino, Raydium, Jupiter, Marginfi, Solend, Marinade, Jito, Meteora, Phoenix, and Mango. The full list with live scores is visible on your dashboard at [app.prflght.xyz](https://app.prflght.xyz).

## Health scores

Each protocol is assigned a `healthScore` from 0 to 100, where 100 represents a fully healthy protocol. Scores are derived from live vault token account balances polled via Helius RPC every 15 minutes. Health data feeds directly into the `POST /check` pipeline - you never need to query it separately.

Each venue also carries a status:

- **`healthy`** - the protocol is operating normally.
- **`warning`** - the protocol's health score has declined to a level that warrants caution.

## How health ties into your policy

**`deny_if_protocol_health_below`** - set an integer threshold (0-100). Any transaction that involves a protocol whose `healthScore` is below this value is denied automatically during the `POST /check` call.

See [Policies](/concepts/policies) for the full policy field reference.
